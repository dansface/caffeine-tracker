Privacy Policy

Browser-only data storage

This caffeine tracker does not transmit your entered tracker data to an application server. Intake history, body weight settings, bedtime preferences, and metabolism options are stored locally in your browser using browser storage technologies such as localStorage.

The page loads Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, and the Chart.js date adapter from the jsDelivr content delivery network. Those requests are separate from your tracker data and may be subject to the providers’ and GitHub Pages’ own privacy policies.

Your tracker information stays on the device you’re using unless you clear browser data or storage manually. We do not collect, transmit, or retain your tracker information server-side. If you use a different browser or device, your saved data will not automatically sync.
